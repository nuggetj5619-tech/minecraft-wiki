Survival

Mining

Learn how to safely explore underground and collect valuable resources.

Bring the Right Equipment

Before entering a cave, bring useful supplies.

  • Pickaxe
  • Torches
  • Food
  • Blocks
  • Weapon
  • Water bucket

Coal

Coal is useful for making torches and as a furnace fuel. It is generally easy to find underground and exposed on the surface.

Iron

Iron is one of the most useful early-game resources. It can be turned into tools, armor, buckets and many other items.

Diamonds

Diamonds are valuable resources used for powerful equipment and several important items.

⚠️ Never dig straight down! You could fall into a cave, lava or another dangerous area. Digging in a staircase or other controlled pattern is much safer.

Mining Safety

  • Place torches so you can find your way home.
  • Keep food in your inventory.
  • Do not carry everything valuable with you.
  • Be careful around lava.
  • Keep extra blocks on your hotbar.

Mining and Exploration

Mining is not only about collecting ores. Underground caves can also contain large amounts of resources and can connect to other areas.
