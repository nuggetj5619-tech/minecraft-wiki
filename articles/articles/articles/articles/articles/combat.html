Survival

Combat

Learn how to defend yourself against Minecraft's hostile mobs.

Know Your Enemies

Different hostile mobs behave differently. Learn what each enemy can do before fighting it.

Common Hostile Mobs

Mob Danger
Zombie Slow melee attacker.
Skeleton Uses a bow from a distance.
Creeper Explodes when it gets close.
Spider Fast melee attacker that can climb.
Enderman Powerful mob that can teleport.

Use a Weapon

Keep a weapon on your hotbar so you can switch to it quickly when enemies appear.

Use Armor

Armor reduces incoming damage and becomes increasingly important as you explore dangerous areas.

Don't Fight Everything

Running away is sometimes the best strategy. If you are carrying valuable items, there is often little reason to take unnecessary risks.

💡 Tip: Keep food nearby during combat so you can recover when you have an opportunity to do so.

Creeper Safety

Creepers are especially dangerous because they can explode and destroy blocks around them. Keep your distance whenever possible.
