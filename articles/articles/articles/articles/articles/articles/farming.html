Farming

Create renewable sources of food and useful resources.

Why Farm?

Farming gives you a renewable supply of resources. A good farm can make survival much easier.

Wheat

Wheat can be grown from seeds and can be used for food-related crafting and breeding animals.

Make a Basic Farm

  1. Find some dirt near water.
  2. Use a hoe to create farmland.
  3. Plant seeds.
  4. Wait for the crops to grow.
  5. Harvest them when ready.

Keep Your Farm Safe

Place fences or other barriers around important farms to prevent animals and other creatures from damaging your crops.

Animal Farming

Animals can provide food and useful resources. Different animals can be bred using different foods.

💡 Tip: Keep your first farm close to your base so you can easily check it while doing other activities.

Expand Your Farm

As your world grows, you can create larger farms and eventually automate parts of your resource production.
