Dimensions

The End

Prepare yourself for one of Minecraft's biggest challenges.

What Is the End?

The End is a separate dimension containing the Ender Dragon and several unique structures and resources.

Prepare First

The End should not be treated like an ordinary exploration trip. Bring strong equipment and enough supplies for a difficult battle.

  • Strong armor
  • A good weapon
  • A bow or other ranged weapon
  • Plenty of food
  • Blocks
  • Useful tools

Finding the End

Reaching the End requires progressing through Minecraft's survival systems and locating the structure containing the End portal.

The Ender Dragon

The Ender Dragon is the primary boss of the End. The fight requires you to pay attention to both the dragon and the environment around you.

⚠️ Important: Do not enter the End while carrying items that you absolutely cannot afford to lose unless you are prepared for the possibility of dying.

After the Dragon

Defeating the Ender Dragon does not mean your Minecraft world is finished. There are still many areas to explore, structures to find and projects to build.

🏆 Congratulations! You've reached the end of the beginner survival path. From here, you can start learning advanced Minecraft systems such as enchanting, redstone, villager trading and large-scale building.
