Dimensions

The Nether

Prepare yourself before entering Minecraft's dangerous Nether dimension.

⚠️ Warning: The Nether is extremely dangerous. Bring good equipment and avoid carrying items you cannot afford to lose.

Finding a Nether Portal

A Nether portal provides a connection between the Overworld and the Nether.

Before You Enter

  • Bring food.
  • Bring strong armor.
  • Bring a weapon.
  • Bring building blocks.
  • Bring a way to navigate.
  • Consider bringing extra equipment.

Nether Mobs

The Nether contains many dangerous creatures. Always pay attention to your surroundings.

Don't Get Lost

It is surprisingly easy to become lost in the Nether. Mark your route and keep track of your portal.

Nether Resources

The Nether contains resources and materials that cannot normally be obtained in the Overworld. Some of these become important for progressing toward the End.

💡 Tip: Build a safe area around your Nether portal so that you have a protected location to return to.
