Crafting

Learn how Minecraft's crafting system works.

What Is Crafting?

Crafting allows you to combine resources to create tools, weapons, blocks, food and many other items.

The 2×2 Crafting Grid

Your inventory contains a small crafting grid. Some simple recipes can be created here.

The Crafting Table

A crafting table provides a 3×3 crafting grid. Most Minecraft recipes require this larger grid.

Important Early Recipes

Item Purpose
Crafting Table Unlocks the 3×3 crafting grid.
Sticks Used in many tools and weapons.
Pickaxe Used for mining.
Axe Used for wood and other blocks.
Sword Used for combat.
Furnace Smelts items and cooks food.

Learn the Recipes

As you play, you can use the recipe book to discover many recipes available to you.

💡 Tip: Don't worry about memorizing every recipe. The recipe book can help you discover what you can craft.
