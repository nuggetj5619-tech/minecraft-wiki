Your First Day

A step-by-step guide to surviving your first Minecraft day and night.

Morning

Start by collecting wood. Around 10–20 logs is a useful amount for getting started.

Make Basic Equipment

  • Crafting table
  • Wooden pickaxe
  • Stone pickaxe
  • Stone sword
  • Stone axe

Find Food

Find a reliable source of food before night. Animals can provide meat, while crops can become a renewable food source later.

Find Sheep

If possible, find three pieces of wool and some wooden planks so you can craft a bed.

💡 Tip: A bed lets you skip the dangerous nighttime period and also establishes a useful respawn point.

Build a Shelter

Your first house does not need to be impressive. A simple wooden or dirt shelter is perfectly fine.

Make sure your shelter has:

  • Walls
  • A roof
  • A door
  • Torches
  • A bed if available

Survive the Night

If you have a bed, sleep through the night. Otherwise, stay inside your shelter and spend the night crafting, organizing items or preparing for your next expedition.

The Next Morning

Once you've survived your first night, you can begin exploring caves, gathering iron and building a permanent base.
