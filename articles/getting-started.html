How to Get Started in Minecraft

Everything you need to know when you're new to Minecraft.

Goal: Your first major goal is to collect resources, make tools, find food and survive your first night.

1. Collect Wood

When you spawn in a new world, the first resource you should collect is wood.

Walk up to a tree and break several logs. Wood is one of the most important resources in the early game because it can be turned into planks, sticks, tools and crafting tables.

2. Make Planks

Open your inventory and place a log into the crafting area.

One log produces several wooden planks. You can use those planks to create many of your first items.

3. Make a Crafting Table

Place four wooden planks into your 2×2 crafting grid to make a crafting table.

The crafting table gives you access to the larger 3×3 crafting grid used for most Minecraft recipes.

4. Make Tools

Your first tools should normally be a pickaxe, axe and sword.

  • Pickaxe — mining blocks and ores
  • Axe — chopping wood
  • Sword — fighting enemies

5. Find Food

You need food to restore your hunger. Animals such as cows, pigs, chickens and sheep can provide early-game food.

6. Find Stone

Use your wooden pickaxe to mine stone. Stone allows you to upgrade from wooden tools to stronger stone tools.

⚠️ Be careful! Do not immediately wander too far from where you spawned. It is easy to get lost before you have a bed or other way to remember your base.

7. Prepare for Night

Minecraft becomes much more dangerous at night. Hostile mobs such as zombies, skeletons and creepers can spawn in dark areas.

Before night arrives, try to have:

  • A shelter
  • Food
  • Tools
  • A weapon
  • Torches
